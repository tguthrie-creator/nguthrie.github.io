Chapter 02 · 2020–2026 · Music

A lifelong practice.

Music has been the constant in my life. I started at the piano as a kid and joined Gilman's Handbell Choir in middle school. Handbells are the most collaborative instrument I know: each ringer holds only a few notes, so a melody exists only when everyone listens to each other.

May 2023 · Gilman Middle School

Three years, one ensemble.

Recognized with Gilman's Three Year Music Ensemble Award for a three-year commitment to Handbell Choir. Showing up to every rehearsal, for every part, was the point.

December 2025 · Piano

Practice as a discipline.

Piano is where I learned patience. Working through a hard passage one measure at a time is the same habit I bring to a tough problem set, and one I expect to lean on in a lab or a clinic.

December 2024 · Concert Band Percussion

Leadership, learned early.

Alongside handbells, I play percussion in Gilman’s Concert Band. In a group this size, no one carries the piece alone. You learn your part, watch the conductor, and listen across the stage so your section fits into the whole. It’s the same habit I rely on everywhere else, do your own job well and stay tuned to the people around you.

Chapter 03 · 2026 · Teamwork

Better together.

On an ultimate frisbee field there usually isn't a referee. Players call their own fouls and settle disagreements on the spot, guided by a principle called Spirit of the Game.

It has taught me to communicate clearly under pressure, to trust my teammates, and to compete hard while staying fair. Those are the same skills I expect to rely on as part of a care team.
